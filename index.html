Football Quiz – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what the Football Quiz app ("Football Quiz", "we", "us") collects, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. Questions: b.balaevi97@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

Your account.

  • Guest: when you play as a Guest, an anonymous account ID is created for you. We do not ask for your name or email.
  • Sign up / log in with email: your email address, used to send you a one-time sign-in code.
  • Sign in with Google: your email address and the basic profile information Google shares when you sign in (your account ID, name and profile picture link).

Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase, which stores this account information for us.

Your profile. The display name you choose (or an automatic "Player <number>" name), the country you choose once, the national team you support, and your favourite club. All of these except the name are optional.

Your game activity. The quizzes you play, your answers and how long each answer took, your points (XP), level, streaks and badges, and when you last opened the app.

Approximate location (optional). If you allow it, Football Quiz reads your phone's approximate (coarse) location only while the app is open, at most once a day. The country is looked up by your phone's own location service (Android's Geocoder; on most phones this is a Google service, which may receive the approximate position for that lookup). Football Quiz itself sends and stores only the 2-letter country code (for example "IT"), one entry per day. It never sends or stores coordinates. If you do not allow location, the app works the same, and the country you chose (if any) is used instead.

Kept only on your phone. Your sign-in session (stored encrypted), copies of screens for offline use, and your daily reminder time. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; we do not use a push-notification service.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, or advertising ID. The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

2. Why we use it

  • To run the game: start quizzes, score your answers, and keep your points, streaks and badges.
  • To keep your progress in your account, so it survives a new phone or a reinstall when you sign in.
  • To show leaderboards. The Country leaderboard uses the country where you spent the most days this month (from approximate location), or the country you chose.
  • To sign you in and send your sign-in code.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • Other players see your display name, your country flag and your rank and points on the leaderboards. They never see your email.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (sign-in and account storage), Google (Sign in with Google, and your phone's location service for the country lookup), and the hosting provider of our server and database. They process data only to provide these services.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account and game data are kept until you delete them.
  • A Guest account that has not opened the app for 180 days is deleted automatically, with all of its data.
  • The daily country entries are kept with your account and deleted with it.

5. Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my data (Guest) or Delete my account (signed in). This deletes your account and all of your game data from our server, including your daily country entries, and removes your sign-in account. You can also ask us by email at b.balaevi97@gmail.com.

To stop location use without deleting anything, turn off the location permission for Football Quiz in your phone's app settings.

6. Children

Football Quiz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we will delete the account.

7. Security

Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your phone. Access to our server and database is restricted. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your data.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
